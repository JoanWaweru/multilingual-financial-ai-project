Kenyan Banks: How to Choose Where to Save or Invest

This is general guidance for evaluating banks. It is not a recommendation of any specific bank.

Key factors to consider:
- Regulation and licensing: confirm the bank is licensed by the Central Bank of Kenya (CBK).
- Deposit protection: understand the Kenya Deposit Insurance Corporation (KDIC) coverage limits.
- Fees and charges: compare account maintenance fees, transfer charges, and ATM fees.
- Interest rates: check savings and fixed deposit rates, and how often interest is paid.
- Access and convenience: branch network, mobile/online banking quality, and customer support.
- Financial stability: review publicly available financial statements and CBK supervision reports.
- Product fit: ensure the account aligns with your goals (short-term liquidity vs fixed deposits).

Good practice:
- Diversify across accounts if balances are large.
- Keep an emergency fund in a liquid savings account.
- Verify terms directly with the bank, as rates and fees change.
