Retirement Planning Basics (Kenya) — Comprehensive Guidance

This document provides general, educational guidance. It is not a recommendation of any specific pension provider or product.

1) Set retirement goals
- Estimate your target retirement age and desired lifestyle.
- Consider expected living costs, healthcare needs, and dependents.

2) Build a long‑term savings habit
- Commit to consistent contributions over time.
- Automate contributions where possible to reduce missed deposits.

3) Understand pension schemes
- Employer schemes may include matching contributions.
- Individual pension plans can supplement employer schemes.
- Rules and benefits vary by provider and scheme type.

4) Risk and time horizon
- Longer horizons can accommodate more volatility.
- Shorter horizons favor stability and capital preservation.

5) Review contributions annually
- Increase contributions as income grows.
- Rebalance if your asset mix drifts from your risk tolerance.

6) Protect your plan
- Maintain an emergency fund to avoid withdrawing retirement savings early.
- Review beneficiaries and documentation regularly.

7) Compliance and verification
- Confirm provider licensing and regulatory oversight.
- Review statements and performance updates.

This guide is for educational purposes only. Always verify terms and rules with licensed providers and official sources.
