Kenya Taxes Core — PAYE, Withholding Tax, KRA Basics

This document provides general, educational guidance only. It does not provide tax advice.

1) PAYE basics
- Employers deduct PAYE from employment income.
- Rates and bands can change; confirm current rates with KRA.

2) Withholding tax
- Applies to certain types of income (e.g., interest, dividends, professional fees).
- Rates and exemptions vary by category and status.

3) Record keeping
- Keep payslips, invoices, and receipts.
- Maintain annual summaries for compliance.

4) Filing and compliance
- Know your filing deadlines and penalties.
- Use official KRA platforms for submissions when required.

Always verify current tax rules and rates with official KRA guidance or licensed tax professionals.
