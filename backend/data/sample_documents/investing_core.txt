Investing Core (Kenya) — MMFs, T‑Bills/Bonds, NSE, ETFs, Dividends

This document provides general, educational guidance only. It does not recommend specific investments or institutions.

1) Money Market Funds (MMFs)
- Pooled funds investing in short‑term, low‑risk instruments.
- Focus on liquidity and capital preservation.
- Returns vary with market conditions and fund policies.

2) Treasury Bills and Bonds
- Government securities issued through the Central Bank of Kenya (CBK).
- T‑Bills are short‑term; T‑Bonds are longer‑term.
- Returns depend on auction rates and market conditions.

3) NSE basics
- Shares represent ownership in listed companies.
- Prices move with company performance and market sentiment.
- Equity investing carries higher volatility and requires risk tolerance.

4) ETFs and funds
- Exchange‑traded funds track baskets of securities.
- Provide diversification and market exposure in one product.
- Fees and tracking accuracy vary by provider.

5) Dividends
- Some companies distribute part of profits to shareholders.
- Dividends are not guaranteed and may change.
- Dividend income should not be the only decision factor.

6) Diversification and risk
- Avoid concentrating all funds in one asset or sector.
- Match your investment choices to your time horizon and risk tolerance.

Always verify current terms, fees, and rules with licensed providers and official sources.
