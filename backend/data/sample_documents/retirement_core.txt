Retirement Planning Core (Kenya) — Pensions and Scheme Types

This document provides general, educational guidance only. It does not recommend specific pension providers.

1) Pension schemes
- Employer schemes may include matching contributions.
- Individual schemes allow voluntary contributions outside employment.
- Benefits and rules vary by provider and scheme type.

2) Regulatory context
- Retirement benefits are overseen by the Retirement Benefits Authority (RBA).
- Always verify provider licensing and scheme registration.

3) Contributions and time horizon
- Consistent, long‑term contributions are critical.
- Longer horizons allow more growth‑oriented allocations.

4) Risk and allocation
- Align risk with your expected retirement timeline.
- Rebalance as you approach retirement to preserve capital.

5) Access and withdrawals
- Withdrawal rules vary by scheme and age requirements.
- Early withdrawals may face penalties or restrictions.

Always verify scheme terms, fees, and withdrawal rules with official sources.
