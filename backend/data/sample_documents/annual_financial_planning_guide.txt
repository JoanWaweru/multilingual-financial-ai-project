Annual Financial Planning (Kenya) — Comprehensive Guidance

This document provides general, educational guidance for Kenyan households and individuals. It is not a recommendation of any specific product or institution. Always verify current terms and rules with licensed providers and official sources.

1) Annual review checklist
- Review last year’s income, spending, and savings.
- Check progress toward goals (emergency fund, education, home deposit, retirement).
- Update your risk tolerance and timeline if circumstances changed.

2) Set annual goals
- Define 1–3 primary goals for the year.
- Convert goals into monthly contributions.
- Prioritize debt reduction if high‑interest liabilities exist.

3) Build a yearly budget
- Use realistic assumptions (income volatility, school fees, medical costs).
- Add a contingency line for unexpected expenses.
- Ensure a dedicated savings and investment line.

4) Emergency fund status
- Maintain 3–6 months of essential expenses.
- Refill the fund immediately after any withdrawal.
- Keep emergency funds liquid and low‑risk.

5) Investment planning
- Decide your annual contribution target.
- Diversify across instruments and providers.
- Rebalance if one asset class has grown disproportionately.

6) Tax and compliance
- Review tax obligations and deadlines.
- Ensure pension contributions or benefits align with applicable rules.
- Verify regulatory status of funds, SACCOs, and providers.

7) Insurance and protection
- Review medical and life cover adequacy.
- Update beneficiaries and contact information.
- Ensure coverage matches dependents and liabilities.

8) Credit health
- Check loan balances and repayment terms.
- Consolidate or refinance only if terms improve overall cost.
- Avoid accumulating high‑interest debt.

9) Record keeping
- Keep annual summaries of accounts and transactions.
- Store receipts and statements securely (digital or physical).
- Maintain a simple net‑worth statement.

10) Year‑end review
- Assess outcomes vs goals.
- Document lessons learned and set next year’s plan.
- Adjust strategy to reflect new realities.

This guide is for educational purposes only. Always confirm current terms and conditions with licensed providers and official sources.
