Insurance Basics (Kenya) — Practical Guidance

This document provides general, educational guidance. It is not a recommendation of any specific insurer or product.

1) Why insurance matters
- It protects you against large, unexpected costs.
- It supports financial stability for you and your dependents.

2) Common types
- Medical insurance: helps cover healthcare costs.
- Life insurance: supports dependents in case of death.
- Property insurance: protects assets like vehicles or property.

3) Coverage fit
- Choose coverage aligned to your dependents, income, and obligations.
- Avoid over‑insuring or under‑insuring.

4) Understand exclusions
- Read policy exclusions and claim conditions.
- Keep documentation and receipts for claims.

5) Review annually
- Update beneficiaries and coverage as life changes.
- Compare policies for better terms when renewing.

This guide is for educational purposes only. Always verify terms and licensing with providers and official sources.
