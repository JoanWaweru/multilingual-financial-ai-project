SME/Business Finance Core (Kenya) — Cashflow, Working Capital, Payroll, Taxes

This document provides general, educational guidance only. It does not recommend specific lenders or services.

1) Cashflow basics
- Track inflows and outflows weekly or monthly.
- Separate business and personal accounts.
- Maintain a cash buffer for slow periods.

2) Working capital
- Monitor inventory, receivables, and payables.
- Reduce cash tied in slow‑moving stock.
- Negotiate payment terms that support liquidity.

3) Payroll planning
- Budget payroll as a fixed monthly obligation.
- Ensure statutory deductions are understood and remitted on time.

4) Business taxes
- Keep accurate records of sales and expenses.
- Know your filing obligations and deadlines.
- Consult official sources for current tax requirements.

Always verify tax obligations and licensing requirements with official sources.
