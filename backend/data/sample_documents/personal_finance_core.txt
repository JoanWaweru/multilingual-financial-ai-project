Personal Finance Core (Kenya) — Budgeting, Emergency Funds, Debt, Savings

This document provides general, educational guidance only. It does not recommend specific institutions or products.

1) Budgeting fundamentals
- Track all income sources and essential expenses.
- Separate needs, wants, and savings.
- Review monthly and adjust based on actual spending.

2) Emergency funds
- Keep 3–6 months of essential expenses in a liquid account.
- Use only for emergencies (medical, job loss, urgent repairs).
- Refill immediately after use.

3) Debt management
- List all debts with balances, interest rates, and due dates.
- Prioritize high‑interest debt or use a structured payoff plan.
- Avoid new high‑cost debt for non‑essential spending.

4) Savings plans
- Set a monthly savings target and automate transfers.
- Save for specific goals separately (education, home deposit, business).
- Use a low‑risk, accessible account for short‑term goals.

5) Good habits
- Pay bills on time to avoid penalties.
- Build a basic net‑worth statement each quarter.
- Revisit your goals at least annually.

Always verify terms and fees with licensed providers and official sources.
