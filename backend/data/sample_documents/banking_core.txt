Banking Core (Kenya) — Account Types, Fees, Digital Banking, SACCO vs Bank

This document provides general, educational guidance only. It does not recommend specific banks.

1) Account types
- Savings accounts: liquid, low‑risk, lower returns.
- Current accounts: transactional, often with fees.
- Fixed deposits: lock‑in for a term with defined returns.

2) Fees and charges
- Review monthly maintenance fees, transfer fees, and ATM charges.
- Compare total costs, not just interest rates.

3) Digital banking
- Evaluate mobile and online banking features.
- Confirm transaction limits and support options.

4) SACCO vs bank
- SACCOs are member‑owned cooperatives; banks are shareholder‑owned institutions.
- SACCOs may offer member loans and dividends; banks offer broader transactional services.
- Rules, membership, and liquidity vary by provider.

Always verify terms and fees with licensed providers and official sources.
