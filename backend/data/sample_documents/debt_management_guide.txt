Debt Management Basics (Kenya) — Practical Guidance

This document provides general, educational guidance. It is not a recommendation of any specific lender or product.

1) Know your total debt
- List all loans, balances, interest rates, and repayment dates.
- Identify high‑interest and short‑term obligations.

2) Prioritize repayments
- Consider focusing on the highest interest debt first.
- Alternatively, pay smaller balances first to reduce the number of accounts.
- Choose a strategy you can maintain consistently.

3) Avoid new high‑cost debt
- Be cautious with short‑term, high‑interest loans.
- Borrow only for essential or income‑generating purposes.

4) Align debt with income stability
- Ensure monthly repayments are sustainable within your budget.
- Avoid over‑committing if income is irregular.

5) Negotiate when needed
- Contact lenders early if you foresee repayment challenges.
- Ask about restructuring or extended repayment terms.

6) Build a buffer
- Maintain an emergency fund to prevent reliance on new debt.

7) Track progress
- Review balances monthly.
- Celebrate milestones to reinforce good habits.

This guide is for educational purposes only. Always verify terms directly with your lender or a licensed advisor.
