Insurance Core (Kenya) — Medical, Life, Motor, Property

This document provides general, educational guidance only. It does not recommend specific insurers or policies.

1) Medical insurance
- Helps cover healthcare costs and hospital bills.
- Check exclusions, waiting periods, and approved providers.

2) Life insurance
- Provides financial support to dependents in case of death.
- Coverage should align with income and dependents’ needs.

3) Motor insurance
- Protects against vehicle damage and third‑party liability.
- Terms vary between comprehensive and third‑party cover.

4) Property insurance
- Protects assets such as homes or business premises.
- Review coverage limits and claim requirements.

5) Policy management
- Review premiums, exclusions, and renewal terms annually.
- Keep documentation for claims.

Always verify terms and licensing with official sources and providers.
