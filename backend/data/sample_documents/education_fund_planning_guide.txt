Education Fund Planning (Kenya) — Practical Guidance

This document provides general, educational guidance. It is not a recommendation of any specific savings or investment product.

1) Define the goal
- Identify the student, level (primary/secondary/university), and expected start date.
- Estimate expected annual costs and inflation impact.

2) Choose a funding timeline
- Short‑term goals favor liquidity and lower risk.
- Long‑term goals allow more growth‑oriented allocations.

3) Plan monthly contributions
- Convert the total target into a monthly contribution plan.
- Use automatic transfers to reduce missed contributions.

4) Separate the fund
- Keep education savings separate from daily spending.
- Avoid using the fund for unrelated expenses.

5) Review annually
- Adjust contributions if costs or timelines change.
- Rebalance risk as the start date approaches.

This guide is for educational purposes only. Always confirm current terms with licensed providers and official sources.
